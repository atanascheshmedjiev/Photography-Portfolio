About

I mostly shoot with medium to long lenses and little light. Most of my favourite frames come from waiting: for the sun to drop, for an animal to turn its head, for two people to forget I am there.

I learned on a Canon 1200D with the 18-55mm f/3.5-5.6 kit lens, photographing whatever stood still long enough. These days it is mostly people: proposals, couples and portraits, plus the animals and landscapes I go looking for between sessions.

I like shadows more than most photographers do. I would rather leave something dark and let one face, one hand or one ridge catch the light than show everything at once.

If you have something coming up that you want photographed properly, write to me and tell me about it.
