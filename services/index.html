Services

Commissions for proposals, couples and portraits. Every shoot starts with a conversation about what you need, so there are no fixed packages here.

Proposals

Who it is for
For anyone planning to ask the question and wanting it photographed without giving the surprise away.
How it works
We plan the spot, the timing and a signal together. I stay out of sight with a long lens, then shoot a short couple session straight after while the light lasts.
What you get
A curated, edited gallery of the proposal and the session that follows, delivered online in full resolution.
Ask about proposals

Couples and engagements

Who it is for
For couples who want relaxed photographs together, at golden hour or after dark.
How it works
A walk-and-talk session at a place that means something to you. Little posing, a lot of movement and light.
What you get
An edited online gallery in full resolution, sized for print and for sharing.
Ask about couples and engagements

Portraits

Who it is for
For people who need a portrait with some character: artists, founders, musicians, or anyone who wants a good photograph of themselves.
How it works
A studio-style or on-location session built around one strong light source.
What you get
A small set of finished portraits, retouched by hand.
Ask about portraits
